Questions

Do I need an account to book?

No. Your name and mobile number are enough. If you verify your number with a one-time code you also get the first-booking discount and can manage your bookings online.

How does “reserve, pay at venue” work?

We hold your slot for 3 hours. Within that time, confirm it by paying online from your booking link or by calling us to pay an advance. Reservations that aren't confirmed are released automatically so others can book.

Can I cancel?

Do you rent rackets and paddles?

Yes, equipment is available at the reception. Ask the team when you arrive.

Can I book for a group or an event?

Yes. Call us and we'll block the courts you need for tournaments, corporate sessions or parties.
